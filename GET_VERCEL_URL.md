# 🔍 How to Get Your Vercel Public URL

From the Vercel deployment page you shared, here's how to find your public URL:

## Method 1: From Deployment Page
1. On the deployment page, look for a **"Visit"** button (usually at the top right)
2. Click it - that's your public URL!

## Method 2: From Project Dashboard
1. Go to your Vercel project dashboard
2. Look at the top - there's a URL displayed (e.g., `tonpay-africa.vercel.app`)
3. Click it or copy it

## Method 3: From Domains Section
1. Go to your project → **Settings** → **Domains**
2. You'll see your public domain listed

## Typical Vercel URLs:
- `https://tonpay-africa.vercel.app`
- `https://tonpay-africa-[random].vercel.app`
- Or a custom domain if you set one up
